Spotify's engineering blog makes the case for treating LLM evaluations as a prerequisite to A/B tests rather than an alternative to them. The idea is to use evals to filter out weak candidates before running experiments, so that only higher-quality variants reach real users. The two methods then form a feedback loop where experiment outcomes help calibrate the eval judges over time.
