GenAI systems are fundamentally probabilistic rather than deterministic, producing different outputs for the same inputs in ways that traditional software doesn't. This distinction requires new development approaches, including evaluation frameworks built around datasets and metrics rather than standard unit tests. The author argues that data scientists and MLOps practitioners already have a head start on this shift, having long worked in environments where uncertainty is the norm rather than the exception.
