On a 10-hour London-to-Las Vegas flight with no wifi, Dmitri Lerko ran Gemma 4 31B and Qwen 4.6 36B on a MacBook Pro M5 Max to build a billing analytics tool and process 4M tokens of smaller tasks. Key limits: sustained GPU heat, battery drain even while plugged in, and a 34W charging shortfall he traced to accidentally using an iPhone cable instead of the MacBook cable.
