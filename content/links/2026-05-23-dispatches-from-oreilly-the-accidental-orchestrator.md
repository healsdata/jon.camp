Andrew Stellman built Octobatch, a batch orchestrator, using AI tools and came away with a framework he calls "agentic engineering." The idea is that experienced developers should act as deliberate orchestrators of AI tools rather than passive recipients of generated code, keeping architectural control and validation throughout.
