As AI handles routine tasks and reduces the need to ask colleagues for help, informal workplace interactions that build trust and psychological safety start to disappear. The article argues these micro-moments of human connection are foundational to team cohesion and creative problem-solving, and that AI adoption erodes them without teams noticing. The authors call for intentional design of team practices to preserve what formal processes cannot replicate.
