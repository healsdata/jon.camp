The author challenges the claim that LLMs represent the next step in the traditional abstraction ladder (binary to assembly to C to Python). The key difference is that every prior abstraction is deterministic and produces only what you asked for, while LLMs generate probabilistic outputs that include unpredictable artifacts beyond the request. That distinction makes LLMs something categorically different, not just a higher rung.
