Structured-Prompt-Driven Development (SPDD) proposes treating prompts as versioned, governed artifacts rather than throwaway inputs. The REASONS Canvas framework organizes the process of capturing business intent before code generation, giving teams a repeatable, auditable approach to LLM-assisted delivery. The methodology aims to make AI-assisted development more consistent and scalable across engineering organizations.
