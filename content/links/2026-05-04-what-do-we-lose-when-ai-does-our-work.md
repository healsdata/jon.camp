The author argues that delegating meaningful work to AI severs the connection between effort and outcome that gives work its meaning. Even as AI reduces friction and speeds up output, the act of delegation hollows out ownership and fulfillment. The prescription is to keep AI in a supporting role for work that actually matters, treating it as a thought partner rather than a replacement.
