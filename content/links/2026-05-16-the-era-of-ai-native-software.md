The article argues that bolting AI onto legacy systems rarely works, and organizations need to treat AI as a foundational architecture decision rather than a feature to add later. It covers how AI-native software differs from retrofitted approaches, including continuous learning, adaptive workflows, and the need to evolve DevOps pipelines with AIOps and MLOps practices. The author frames CI/CD evolution as a shift from code deployment to intelligence deployment, where model retraining and validation are built into the pipeline alongside application changes.
