title: "AI Self-preferencing in Algorithmic Hiring: Empirical Evidence and Insights"
date: 2026-05-02T00:00:00-05:00
published: 2025-08-30T00:00:00-05:00
link: "https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.00462"
---

Researchers tested whether LLMs favor resumes they generated over human-written ones, and found consistent self-preference bias ranging from 67 to 82 percent across models. Candidates whose resumes matched the evaluating LLM were 23 to 60 percent more likely to advance in screening. The effect was strongest in business roles, suggesting that as AI hiring tools spread, human-written applications face a growing structural disadvantage.
