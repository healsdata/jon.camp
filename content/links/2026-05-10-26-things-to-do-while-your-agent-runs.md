A list of suggested activities to fill idle time while AI agents run, organized by wait duration from a few seconds up to about 12 minutes. Suggestions range from reviewing prompts and hydrating in short gaps to planning verification steps or taking real breaks in longer ones. The underlying advice is to stay ahead of the agent rather than staring at the screen or doomscrolling.
