Widespread AI tool adoption at the individual level doesn't automatically translate into organizational learning. While individuals gain productivity, companies struggle to convert those gains into systematic improvements. The author frames three missing capabilities needed to close the gap: visibility into what AI agents are doing, understanding of which AI-assisted workflows actually create value, and mechanisms for distributing useful patterns across teams.
