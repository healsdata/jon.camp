GitHub built an experimental accessibility agent that reviews pull requests for accessibility issues and proposes fixes. After 3,535 PRs reviewed at a 68% resolution rate, the team shares what worked - using sub-agents to break up complex tasks and training on manually curated examples rather than relying on the model's general accessibility knowledge.
