Stripe introduces a payment mechanism for AI agents built on its Link wallet and a new Issuing for Agents product. Agents can make purchases using one-time-use virtual cards or Shared Payment Tokens without accessing raw card credentials. Consumers authorize and monitor agent spending through a web or mobile app.
