TL;DR: "Private by obscurity" has been dissolved. Agents can now reverse-engineer entire codebases and exploit internal platform APIs that were never meant to be public, bypassing the implicit conventions that kept human developers from misusing them. The author argues platform teams need explicit technical guardrails and clear machine-readable boundaries rather than relying on norms alone.
