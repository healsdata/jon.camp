Unlike traditional software, AI systems produce variable and uncertain outputs, which requires product managers to rethink how they design workflows and user experiences. This guide covers strategies including structuring workflows for verification, being transparent about uncertainty, and incorporating human oversight at key decision points. The author also recommends constraining AI to bounded problem spaces rather than building open-ended general assistants.
