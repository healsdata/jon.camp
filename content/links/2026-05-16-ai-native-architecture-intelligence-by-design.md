AI-native architecture treats intelligence as a core system component designed in from the start, rather than bolted on as an afterthought. The author outlines design principles for building these systems, covering continuous learning loops, data pipelines, model lifecycle management, and governance. The argument is that teams building on generative AI need these foundations in place to create platforms that are genuinely scalable and adaptive over time.
