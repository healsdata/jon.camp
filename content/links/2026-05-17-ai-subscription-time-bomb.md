AI companies have been subsidizing subscription pricing to drive adoption, but that model cannot hold. Token consumption from agentic workflows is growing fast, and as these companies approach IPOs, pricing will need to reflect actual costs. Enterprises that built AI into their workflows without auditing spend could face serious budget surprises when the subsidies end.
