An Nvidia executive acknowledged that AI deployment currently costs more than employing human workers for equivalent tasks. Some companies aren't treating the higher price tag as a drawback, framing it as a long-term investment rather than an operational liability. The article covers the reasoning behind that position and the broader trend of businesses accepting elevated AI costs.
