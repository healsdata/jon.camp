The author argues that AI is unlikely to accelerate software delivery, drawing on the historical pattern that no major wave of tooling has reliably done so. New languages, frameworks, and methodologies all promised faster delivery and largely didn't. The article questions why AI would be the exception rather than another iteration of the same pattern.
