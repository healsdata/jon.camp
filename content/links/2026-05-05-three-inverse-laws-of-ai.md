A riff on Asimov proposing three principles for living with AI systems: avoid attributing human qualities to them, verify their output rather than accepting it blindly, and retain full responsibility for any decisions made using AI. The underlying concern is that as AI becomes more embedded in daily work, uncritical acceptance of its output is the main failure mode to guard against.
